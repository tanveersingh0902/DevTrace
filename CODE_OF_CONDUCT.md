# DevTrace Code of Conduct

DevTrace adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its pledge, standards, scope, and enforcement guidelines apply in project spaces and when representing DevTrace elsewhere.

Welcome people across backgrounds and experience levels. In reviews, identify the specific issue, explain its effect, and give the author room to respond. Harassment, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone's private information violate this standard.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report an incident privately to a maintainer using a contact method they have published on their GitHub profile. If the concern involves that maintainer, or no private contact is listed, GitHub's reporting tools provide a separate route. Keep identifying information and evidence out of public issues. [GitHub reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)

Maintainers handle reports discreetly, consider the impact and context, and apply the Covenant's enforcement guidelines. Actions can include a correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban. Maintainers may remove harmful content and explain moderation decisions to the people involved when appropriate.
